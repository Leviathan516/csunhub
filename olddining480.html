Top Places to Eat Near CSUN

Looking for a good bite between classes? Here are some favorite popular spots on and around campus.

SPOTS NEAR HOUSING

G'mos

Located in Arbor Court, the Arbor Grill offers snacks, sandwiches, burgers, pizza, and salads. It’s a convenient on-campus option when you don’t want to stray far.

Insider tip: It’s busiest right after classes, so try going just before or after peak times.

Matador Food Court (On Campus)

The Matador Food Court contains familiar favorites like Burger King, Subway, El Pollo Loco, and Panda Express. Ideal for groups where everyone wants something different.

Freudian Sip (Campus)

A CSUN-original café, Freudian Sip has multiple campus locations serving coffee, pastries, sandwiches, and grab-and-go items.

El Torito (Northridge)

A short drive from campus, El Torito offers Mexican cuisine in a sit-down setting. Great for group lunches or dinner.

Black Angus Steakhouse (Northridge)

Known for its steaks and hearty meals, Black Angus is a solid pick when you're looking to splurge a little.

Emle’s

Among students’ go-to picks is Emle’s — affordable, wide menu, and good portions. A dependable off-campus meal spot.

My Hero Sandwiches

This sandwich shop just off campus is often praised by students for being affordable and tasty.
